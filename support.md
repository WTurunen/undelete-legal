# Support

Send a support request by email to
**[{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }})**.

For a security vulnerability, use
[{{ site.security_email }}](mailto:{{ site.security_email }}) instead, as the
[security page](security) describes.

## Hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00 Finnish time (Europe/Helsinki), except Finnish public
holidays. These are the hours when we read and answer support requests.

We aim to answer within 48 hours. Weekends and Finnish public holidays are not counted.

## What to include

- Your Jira site URL, for example `your-company.atlassian.net`.
- The issue key, if the problem concerns one issue, for example `ABC-123`.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- The text of any error message. A screenshot is fine if it shows only the error.

**Do not send customer data.** Do not paste the content of issues, comments or files,
and do not send screenshots that show them. Issue keys and error messages are enough to
start.

## Before you write

- [Documentation](docs): how to set up and use the app.
- [Known limitations](limitations): what the app cannot restore, and why.
